Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This is sample text and must be reviewed before real use.

This policy explains what information Spendly collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password (stored in a protected, hashed form).
  • Expense data: the expenses you enter, including amounts, dates, categories and any notes you add.
  • Basic technical data: such as your browser type and the pages you visit, used to keep the Service running.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To store your expenses and show you category breakdowns and monthly summaries.
  • To reply when you contact us.
  • To keep the Service secure and fix problems.

3. Data Storage & Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, such as hashing passwords and limiting who can access stored data. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Cookies

We use a small number of essential cookies, for example to keep you signed in. We do not use cookies for advertising.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your data. We only share information when the law requires it, or with trusted service providers (such as hosting) who help us run Spendly and must keep your data private.

6. Your Rights

You can ask us to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that is wrong or out of date.
  • Delete your account and your data.

To make a request, please contact us.

7. Data Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is open. If you delete your account, we delete your personal data within a reasonable time, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

8. Children’s Privacy

Spendly is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has given us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date above.

10. Contact Us

Questions about your privacy? Email support@spendly.example or use our contact form.
